4px grid, square corners, hard shadow

Spacing steps

Component padding s3–s4, block gaps s6–s8, section rhythm s12 / 64 / 48 / 32. --space-5 (20px) exists for the token contract and is not used.

Edges and elevation

radius 0
--elev-ring
--elev-raised
--focus-ring

Every box is square. Hover on primary buttons and cards adds the hard 4px ink offset and moves the element 1px up-left. No blur, gradient, glass or glow.

Spine and container

Page or card spine

10px of --tdna-spine yellow on the left edge, once per page or per card. Hub cards swap the bar colour per card via --card-bar.

Container

--container-max 1100px (admin 1200px). Gutters 32 / 24 / 16px. Phones drop the outer margin and keep the spine; tables scroll inside .tbl, the page never scrolls sideways.
